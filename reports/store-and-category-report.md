# Store and category performance report

**UK FMCG store network · 52 weeks ended 28 December 2025 · Simulated data**

## Store exceptions

Twenty-six branches missed their individual sales targets. The three largest cash shortfalls accounted for **£521k**, or **23.9%** of the £2.18m shortfall across below-target stores.

| Branch | Net sales | Sales target | Cash variance | Vs target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lidl · South · Large | £2,358,579 | £2,541,106 | −£182,528 | −7.2% |
| Waitrose · London · Large | £3,095,567 | £3,271,122 | −£175,555 | −5.4% |
| Asda · Scotland · Standard | £2,032,861 | £2,196,142 | −£163,281 | −7.4% |

**London shows why sales rank and target performance answer different questions.** The region beat target by £1.52m, but the Tesco London Large branch missed its target by £103,211 despite leading the region in sales at £5.00m. The Waitrose London Large branch also missed its target. These were London’s only two shortfalls.

| Store format | Branches | Net sales | Sales per branch | Vs target | Branches below target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Large | 25 | £77.76m | £3.11m | +1.6% | 10 |
| Standard | 25 | £54.29m | £2.17m | +3.7% | 8 |
| Compact | 25 | £38.15m | £1.53m | +3.9% | 8 |

Large branches generated **45.7% of sales** and had the narrowest margin above plan. A branch review should start with cash gaps, then compare against branches of the same format. The simulated data can show which weeks and categories explain the variance. A real review would need additional evidence to establish causes such as availability or staffing.

## Category economics

| Category | Net sales | Gross profit | Gross margin | Promo sales share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Chilled and dairy | £34.24m | £7.64m | 22.3% | 18.2% |
| Ambient grocery | £33.32m | £8.80m | 26.4% | 18.2% |
| Fresh produce | £31.33m | £6.65m | 21.2% | 18.1% |
| Beverages | £19.81m | £5.22m | 26.3% | 18.2% |
| Household and personal care | £18.86m | £6.33m | 33.6% | 18.2% |
| Bakery | £17.07m | £5.57m | 32.6% | 18.1% |
| Frozen | £15.59m | £3.78m | 24.2% | 18.2% |

**Chilled and dairy led on sales, while ambient grocery led on gross profit.** Ambient generated £8.80m in gross profit, about £1.16m more than chilled and dairy, on about £0.92m less revenue. Household and personal care had the highest margin rate at 33.6%. Use both gross profit and margin rate when deciding which categories to investigate.

**Promo sales share is nearly uniform.** The category figures round to 18.1% or 18.2%, reflecting the common promotion assumptions in the simulation. The metric measures share of revenue sold at a promotional price. Estimating incremental lift would require a comparable baseline or control.
