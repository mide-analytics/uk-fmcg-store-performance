# Executive trading report

**UK FMCG store network · 52 weeks ended 28 December 2025 · Simulated data**

The 75-branch network delivered **£170.21m in net sales**, up **6.9%** from the matched prior year and **£4.61m above target**. All five retailer banners beat plan at the aggregate level. At branch level, **26 of 75 stores missed their own targets**, with a combined **£2.18m shortfall** across those stores.

## What the results mean

- **Tesco and Asda drove 56.6% of the network’s year-over-year sales increase.** Together they generated £85.40m in sales, about half the network total. Their performance carries more weight in the aggregate than the other banners.
- **The regional spread matters for follow-up.** London finished £1.52m above plan (+4.0%), while the North finished only £0.10m ahead (+0.3%). Seven of the North’s 15 branches missed target, the highest count among the five regions.
- **The final five weeks accounted for 12.1% of annual sales.** The week starting 1 December was the highest at £4.14m. The simulation includes a seasonal uplift in these weeks, so use the peak to illustrate how the report surfaces seasonal concentration.
